About Amicus

A decade of helping families find a home in Canada.

Amicus Immigration Consultants Canada Inc. is a Mississauga-based firm that has spent over ten years guiding foreign workers, skilled tradespeople and their families through the Canadian immigration system — with affordable fees and a kind, considerate approach.

Meet the Founder

Dinshaw Engineer

Founder & Principal Consultant, Amicus Immigration Consultants Canada Inc.

Dinshaw has spent more than a decade helping people from around the world build new lives in Canada. He started Amicus on a simple idea: that immigration support should be honest, affordable and rooted in real human understanding — not a high-priced black box.

His clients keep coming back (and sending their friends and family) because he treats every file as if it were his own family’s. He knows Canadian immigration policy inside and out, but what people remember is his patience, his straight talk, and how he’ll happily get on a call at odd hours when someone is anxious about a deadline.

Dinshaw lives in the Greater Toronto Area and works with employers and workers across all ten Canadian provinces and three territories.

What We Stand For

Our commitments to every client

The values below aren’t marketing copy — they’re the rules we run the practice by.

💰

Economical fees

We quote one fair, transparent fee for your whole file. No surprises. No drip pricing. If a program isn’t right for you, we won’t sell it to you.

🧡

Considerate & kind

You’re trusting us with your life plan. We treat that trust with patience, warmth and respect — at every step.

📚

Deep expertise

Ten-plus years of hands-on work with IRCC, ESDC and provincial nominee programs. We know the rules and how they really play out.

🌍

The whole country, not just one city

We help you find the right job and the right region — coast to coast — for the life your family actually wants.

📝

Straight talk

If your odds are slim, we tell you. If there’s a better route, we recommend it — even if it means a smaller fee for us.

✅

Licensed & accountable

Authorized to represent you under Canadian immigration regulations, with a clear duty of care that we take seriously.
