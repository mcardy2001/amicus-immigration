Already in Canada? Let’s keep you working.

If you’re a nanny, home child-care provider or personal support worker already here, staying employed and in status is what matters most. We help you:

Bringing a nanny to Canada — including from the Philippines

The Philippines is where many Canadian families find experienced, dedicated caregivers. Hiring from overseas takes some coordination, and we manage the pipeline end to end:

  • Your LMIA application as the household employer, built to caregiver-specific requirements
  • The work permit for your chosen nanny or caregiver
  • A clear path to permanent residence for the worker — often the deciding factor in attracting the right person
  • The overseas steps: there’s also a process on the Philippine side for workers leaving to work abroad, and we’ll make sure you know what’s required so your hire doesn’t stall

Common Questions

Caregiver & nanny FAQs

Can I hire a nanny from the Philippines?

Often, yes — usually through an LMIA-backed job offer and a work permit for the worker, with additional steps on the Philippine side. We start with a free assessment of whether your household and the role qualify.

I’m a caregiver in Canada — can I get PR?

There are dedicated permanent-residence pathways for caregivers with Canadian experience, including the Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots. Eligibility depends on your work history, job offer and language results.

Do I need an LMIA to hire a caregiver?

In most cases a household hiring a foreign caregiver needs a positive LMIA before the worker applies for a permit. Caregiver roles have their own requirements, and we build the LMIA to meet them.

How long does it take?

Timelines vary with the program, the LMIA queue and where the worker applies from. We give you a realistic estimate at the consultation — not a one-size-fits-all number.
