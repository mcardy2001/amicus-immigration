What We Do

Bringing skilled hands to a country that needs them

We match foreign workers with employers in industries where Canada needs help most — and guide both sides through every step of the immigration process.

⚒

Construction & Trades

Carpenters, framers, electricians, welders, heavy-equipment operators and general labourers placed with reputable Canadian builders.

🍽

Hotels & Hospitality

Front-desk staff, housekeeping, cooks, food and beverage workers, and supervisors for hotels and resorts across Canada.

🍔

Restaurants & Food Service

Line cooks, kitchen helpers, servers and shift supervisors for quick-service and full-service restaurants nationwide.

📄

LMIA & Work Permits

Full Labour Market Impact Assessment preparation and employer-specific or open work permit applications.

🏠

Permanent Residence

Pathways through Express Entry, PNPs, the Atlantic Immigration Program and the Rural Community Immigration Pilot.

👪

Family Sponsorship

Bring your spouse, children or parents to join you once you’re established in your new Canadian home.
