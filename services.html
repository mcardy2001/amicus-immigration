Where We Place Workers

Industries that need skilled, reliable people

Canada faces persistent labour shortages in trades, hospitality and food service. We help foreign workers fill these roles — with employer partners we trust to treat them well.

⚒

Construction & Skilled Trades

Canada is in the middle of a multi-decade construction boom — housing, infrastructure, energy projects and commercial builds. Demand for skilled hands far outstrips local supply.

  • Carpenters, framers, drywall installers and finishing trades
  • Electricians, plumbers, gas fitters and HVAC technicians
  • Welders, ironworkers and structural metal trades
  • Heavy-equipment operators and crane operators
  • Concrete formwork, masonry and general construction labourers
  • Site supervisors and project foremen
🍽

Hotels & Hospitality

From boutique mountain resorts in BC and Alberta to large city-centre hotels in Toronto, Montreal and Halifax — Canadian hospitality employers are actively hiring abroad.

  • Housekeeping room attendants and laundry staff
  • Front desk agents and night auditors
  • Banquet servers, bartenders and food & beverage staff
  • Hotel cooks, chefs de partie and pastry cooks
  • Maintenance technicians and grounds staff
  • Supervisors and assistant managers
🍔

Restaurants & Food Service

Quick-service chains, family restaurants and fine-dining kitchens across Canada rely on dedicated international workers to keep their doors open.

  • Line cooks, prep cooks and kitchen helpers
  • Bakers and specialty food preparers
  • Servers, hosts and counter attendants
  • Shift supervisors and assistant managers
  • Dishwashers and food service helpers
🚚

Trucking, Warehousing & Logistics

Long-haul drivers, warehouse associates, forklift operators and dispatch staff for transportation and supply-chain employers nationwide.

📱

Caregiving & Personal Support

Pathways for personal support workers and home child-care providers, including the Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots.

Immigration Pathways

The programs we work in every day

Different programs suit different people. Here’s an overview of the routes we navigate on your behalf.

📄

LMIA-Based Work Permits

Labour Market Impact Assessment preparation for employers, plus the closed work permit application for the worker. The most common route for trades and hospitality roles.

🛡

LMIA-Exempt Permits

Intra-company transfers, CUSMA/CETA professionals, international agreements and other streams that don’t require an LMIA.

🏠

Express Entry & PR

Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class and Federal Skilled Trades — including category-based draws for trades and healthcare.

🌍

Provincial Nominee Programs

Tailored nomination streams in Ontario, Alberta, BC, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, New Brunswick and beyond — often the fastest route to PR.

🌍

Atlantic Immigration Program

Employer-driven PR for designated employers in Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, PEI and Newfoundland & Labrador.

🏘

Rural & Francophone Pilots

Pathways for smaller communities — including the Rural Community Immigration Pilot — that match employers in lower-cost regions with skilled foreign workers.

👪

Family Sponsorship

Once you’re a permanent resident, we help you sponsor your spouse, children, parents and grandparents to join you.

🎓

Study Permits & PGWP

For workers whose path runs through Canadian credentials — study permit applications and post-graduation work permit support.

Coast to Coast

Finding the right region for your role and your life

Canada is enormous — and the right job in the wrong region can be the wrong job. We help you weigh climate, cost of living, schools, community and career growth.

Atlantic Canada

Ontario & Quebec

Prairies
